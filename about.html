About Student Hub

Our mission

Student Hub is a campus portal that brings student information and events together in one accessible place.

Who can use it?

  • Guests can view public pages and register.
  • Students can view their dashboard and manage their profile.
  • Admins can manage students, events and users.

Quick facts

Project type
Semester-long web application
Technologies
HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, PHP, MySQL
